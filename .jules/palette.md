## 2024-07-09 - Missing accessibility patterns in sibling auth components
**Learning:** Sibling components within the same section (e.g., auth pages like login and signup) often have inconsistent accessibility implementations, such as missing `sr-only` labels or missing loading states.
**Action:** When working on a form component, consistently cross-reference its sibling components to ensure standardized UX and accessibility practices (like `sr-only` labels, linked `id`s, and `role="alert"`) are applied.
