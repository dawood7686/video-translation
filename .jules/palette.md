## 2024-07-06 - Consistent Accessibility Across Sibling Forms
**Learning:** Sibling forms (like login and signup) often diverge in accessibility implementations. The signup page lacked sr-only labels, input IDs, and loading states present in the login form.
**Action:** Always cross-reference sibling components when adding or modifying forms to ensure standardized accessibility practices (like sr-only labels and linked IDs) and interaction feedback are maintained.
